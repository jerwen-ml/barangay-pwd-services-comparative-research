# Publication and Privacy Checklist

This GitHub repository is **public** but contains only a **high-level academic portfolio summary**. Public visibility does **not** mean permission has been established to distribute the full study.

Before adding the full group manuscript or supplementary research material:

- [ ] Confirm that **both named co-researchers** agree to the proposed public release and attribution.
- [ ] Check with the CTU research adviser/institution about publication rules, intellectual property, and any restrictions on student research.
- [ ] Review participant consent and ethical approvals for the specific type of online disclosure.
- [ ] Remove direct identifiers and scrutinize indirect identifiers in a small community.
- [ ] Do not upload names, phone numbers, signatures, questionnaires, interview audio, transcripts, or individual response sheets.
- [ ] Review any quotations or small-group tables for re-identification risk even if names are omitted.
- [ ] Recheck aggregate calculations, literature references, and citations before expanding the public summary.
- [ ] Agree on reuse terms with all rights holders **before** adding a license.

**Important:** Removing a file from a public GitHub repository does not necessarily remove it from forks, caches, or history. Share only material that has been approved for public release.

## Current repository policy

**Allowed here:** High-level overview, generalized methodology, non-identifying findings summary, and this checklist.

**Not yet included:** Full manuscript, participant-level data, original research worksheets, interview records, raw surveys, consent forms, or other unpublished attachments.
