# Methodology — Public Portfolio Summary

**Source:** The researchers' comparative group manuscript. This page summarizes its stated procedures, without independently verifying primary records.

## Design and setting

The research applied a **comparative, barangay-based mixed-methods design** in:
- Barangay Dagohoy, Inabanga, Bohol; and
- Barangay Don Andres Soriano (DAS), Toledo City, Cebu.

The final manuscript reports 60 survey respondents and seven stakeholder interview participants in each barangay (120 surveys and 14 interviews altogether). It also discusses open-ended survey responses and field observations.

## Quantitative methods

The instrument contained 20 effectiveness statements organized into five four-item domains, plus challenge/barrier items and selected service-awareness questions. The manuscript used frequency counts, percentages, weighted scores, means, and descriptive cross-barangay comparisons.

## Qualitative methods

Stakeholder and open-ended responses were coded, grouped into categories, and synthesized into themes. The authors integrated those interpretations with survey results and observations.

## Limitations stated in the manuscript

- A formal probability-sampling frame was not documented.
- No inferential statistical tests were reported; numerical differences should not be described as statistically significant.
- Field-observation indicators were not identical across the two settings.
- Survey recognition of services does not establish a complete, independently verified inventory of public programs.

## Privacy

Individual-level data, interview transcripts, private quotations, consent materials, and research recruitment information are excluded from this public portfolio. These materials must not be uploaded without separate privacy, ethics, and authorship review.
