## Research title

## About the project

This CTU research project examined community perceptions of the effectiveness, availability, accessibility, responsiveness, and inclusiveness of services for persons with disabilities (PWDs) in two barangays in Central Visayas, Philippines.

It used a **comparative mixed-methods** design that brought together surveys, stakeholder interviews, open-ended responses, and field observations.

| Study component | Scope |
|---|---|
| Locations | Barangay Dagohoy (Bohol) and Barangay Don Andres Soriano / DAS (Cebu) |
| Survey respondents | **120 total** (60 per barangay) |
| Interview participants | **14 total** (7 per barangay) |
| Quantitative methods | Frequencies, percentages, weighted means, descriptive comparisons |
| Qualitative methods | Coding, thematic synthesis, and integration with survey findings |

## Overall findings

The manuscript describes relatively similar overall perceived effectiveness ratings across the two barangays, alongside persistent service-delivery concerns, including the continuity of programs, access to information, resource allocation, PWD participation, and monitoring. These are **research participants' reported perceptions**, not an audit of government performance or proof of statistically significant differences.
