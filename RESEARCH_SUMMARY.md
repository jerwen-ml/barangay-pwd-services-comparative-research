# Research Findings — Public Overview

**Source:** The comparative CTU group manuscript provided by the researchers. This is an educational portfolio summary, **not** an independent audit or formal publication.

## Study composition

The research covered two barangays. Each contributed 60 survey respondents and seven stakeholder interview participants, producing **120 survey respondents** and **14 interview participants** in total.

## General results

The study describes similar overall participant ratings of the effectiveness of PWD-related barangay services across the two locations. It also identifies concerns requiring attention, including:
- Regularity and continuity of PWD-focused activities and services
- Accessible information and referral pathways
- Adequate resources and staff capacity
- Participation of persons with disabilities in community activities
- Clear monitoring and evaluation procedures

These observations represent the study's survey and qualitative evidence. They are not definitive rankings of the two barangays or claims about any particular official.

## Program enhancement themes

The researchers discussed strengthening accessible information, coordination among services, staff preparation, inclusive community engagement, and routine program monitoring as possible priorities.

## Analysis boundaries

The manuscript used **descriptive statistics** and thematic analysis. It did not report statistical significance testing or a causal evaluation. Program-enhancement suggestions were proposed rather than experimentally tested.

## Materials deliberately withheld

No respondent-level dataset, identifiable quotations, transcripts, raw questionnaires, consent documents, or full manuscript is included in this repository. Full-paper sharing requires both authors' agreement and any relevant CTU/adviser and participant-privacy clearances.
